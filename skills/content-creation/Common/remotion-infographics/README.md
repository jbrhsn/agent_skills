# Remotion Infographics

Turn an article, draft, `source.md`, or post idea into a 10–30 second Remotion infographic. After the creative direction is confirmed, the skill creates an editable `remotion-infographic/` workspace beside the content and delivers `out/infographic.mp4` plus `out/infographic-hero.png`, rendered from the exact final video frame.

The workflow first researches current platform patterns and examples for the intended audience and destination, separating observed popularity, platform guidance, creator advice, and measured retention. It then presents at least five style directions, recommends one with direct reasoning and limits, offers theme and palette options, and asks for approval or revisions. A supplied palette, approved direction, or explicit delegation is enough to proceed.

The six adaptable treatments are kinetic editorial, data spotlight, before/after split, blueprint flow, paper cutout explainer, and modular card stack. The selected style uses a meaningful first frame, useful visual progress, a readable muted experience, and a settled final 2–3 second hero. It preserves the source’s message, factual scope, opinions, voice, and uncertainty; it does not invent data or promise retention or virality.

The project follows Remotion’s frame-driven rendering model and includes source components, configuration, pinned dependencies, a lockfile, project README, research links, asset credits, and reproduction commands. The workflow updates the containing project’s `.gitignore` with the requested `**/remotion-graphic` rules and matching `remotion-infographic/` rules, then verifies the local workspace is ignored without removing tracked files.

Before delivery, it type-checks and renders both artifacts, inspects key frames and playback where available, and checks metadata, readable safe areas, factual labels, audio and captions where present, and final-frame parity.
