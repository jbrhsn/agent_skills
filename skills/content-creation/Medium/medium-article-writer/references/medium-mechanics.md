# Medium packaging and policy

Platform requirements change. Verify current guidance when publication eligibility, disclosures, formatting limits, or monetization matter; record the date and link in publishing notes. If unavailable, identify the unresolved check instead of inventing a rule.

## Policy reference

Checked 2026-09-19: Medium's [AI-content policy](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-content-policy) distinguishes generated writing from limited assistive uses. It restricts paywall eligibility for AI-generated writing and calls for disclosure of generated text and captions identifying generated images. Its FAQ exempts outlining, fact verification, and spelling/grammar assistance from disclosure. Recheck the live policy for the actual workflow and intended publication; editing for a natural voice does not establish eligibility.

Provide accurate disclosure wording for the assistance actually used, and flag applicable paywall/review decisions in publishing notes. Do not describe mostly generated work as mere spell-checking. Preparing an article does not certify its eligibility.

Consult [Medium's quality/distribution guidance](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution) and any chosen publication's current submission rules. Do not guarantee Boost, earnings, or audience reach.

## Packaging

Offer title alternatives only when useful, with meaningful differences rather than a fixed quota. A subtitle can add scope or stakes without repeating the title. Tags should accurately describe the piece within the current editor limit; no unsupported priority-order rule or mandatory broad/narrow ratio.

A publication suggestion needs current evidence of fit and submission availability. Do not invent acceptance odds or claim follower-count thresholds determine success.

Check the actual editor preview after pasting: title/subtitle, headings, code, lists, links, image crop, captions, and alt text. Interface labels may change, so avoid assuming a particular toolbar sequence is timeless. Do not create public code embeds without authorization.

## Performance after publication

Use the author's available analytics, relevant baselines, traffic sources, and observation windows. Low click-through may reflect topic, audience, placement, sample size, or packaging; it does not uniquely diagnose a bad title. Recommend bounded experiments instead of universal CTR targets.
