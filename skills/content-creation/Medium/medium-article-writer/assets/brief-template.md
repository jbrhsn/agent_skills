One sentence. The specific claim or story this piece makes — not the topic.

Topic: "Iceberg compaction." Angle: "Our compaction job was the cost problem, not

## Reader

Who this is for and what they already know. One or two lines. Be narrow — "data
engineers running Iceberg at scale who've noticed their storage bill climbing"
beats "developers."

## Promise

What the reader can do or decide after finishing. If this cannot be stated
concretely, the piece isn't ready to write.

structure from `article-structures.md`.
