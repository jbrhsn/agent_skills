# Publishing notes

Keep notes separate from the publishable copy. Complete the selection, publishing recommendations, and readiness fields; explicitly recommend none when tags or media would not help. Remove instructional placeholders from the delivered file.

## Assignment

**Draft:** Path or inline version.

**Source:** Source/version and relevant optional context.

**Audience and purpose:** Intended reader and value; pillar only when known.

**Format:** Short, medium-length, or long single post, or thread; explain a consequential choice.

## Confirmed direction and selection

Selected hook and structure, author confirmation, and incorporated suggestions; record only what actually happened. Briefly record the five proposals and their comparison. Explain why the recommendation fits the author's message, ideas, voice, and style and the researched performance patterns. Link research sources with dates, available metrics, limitations, and confidence; label editorial judgment when evidence is insufficient.

## Evidence and editorial choices

Sources, dates, supported claims, unresolved claims, and material omissions or reframing. Distinguish author experience, external evidence, and illustrative material.

## Publishing recommendations

**Time:** Recommended day/time or window, audience timezone, and rationale from analytics or relevant evidence; otherwise label it an experiment and state assumptions or missing audience information. No guaranteed reach windows.

**Tags and mentions:** Relevant hashtags and suggested account mentions that fit the author's style, or an explicit recommendation of none. Verify account identities and count any tags included in the copy.

**Images and media:** Recommended asset, its purpose, placement or thread post number, caption/credit, and accessibility needs, or why no media is needed. Distinguish proposed assets and provisional alt text from inspected media. No article-style subtitle is required.

**Links and close:** Link placement and CTA if useful; no assumed link penalty or forced engagement request.

## Fit and readiness

Account/client assumptions where relevant. Final length or per-post lengths, counting method, and whether fit is verified or still needs a composer check. Include numbering and links in published-text counts.

Verify fidelity to the author's message, ideas, voice, style, qualifications, and attribution. Resolve essential factual gaps and remaining author suggestions; check links, mentions, media credits, accessibility, publishing time/timezone, and the actual composer preview. For threads, confirm sequence and each post's independent fit. Record remaining blockers honestly. Preparing copy does not publish or schedule it.
