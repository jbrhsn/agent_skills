# LinkedIn Post Writer

Draft or revise LinkedIn posts from notes, supplied text, or an existing draft. The skill preserves the author's meaning, voice, uncertainty, and attribution while improving structure and reader value.

After author confirmation, the default drafting workflow produces two files beside the source: `linkedin_post.md` with only the paste-ready post and `linkedin_publish.md` with publishing notes. The notes include the selection rationale and sources, a recommended publishing time and audience timezone, relevant tags or mentions, and checks before publishing. Explicitly inline requests and narrow edits need no extra artifact set. An existing `source.md` is a convention, not a platform-selection rule.

For a new draft or substantial rewrite, the skill first generates five proposals, each pairing a hook with a full-post outline. It then researches relevant LinkedIn performance evidence, compares the proposals, and recommends the closest supported fit while preserving the author's message, ideas, voice, and style. It presents the options, source-linked reasoning, and limitations, then waits for author confirmation and suggestions before writing the full post or output files. Narrow edits and already confirmed directions do not restart this review.

Research distinguishes measured results from advice and anecdotes. If web access or credible evidence is unavailable, the skill discloses the gap and labels its recommendation as editorial judgment. There is no mandatory character band, emoji quota, or first-comment link rule. Facts and personal experiences are never invented.

Detailed guidance covers [voice](references/voice-inference.md), [openings](references/hook-frameworks.md), [structure](references/structure-and-format.md), and [distribution evidence](references/algorithm-mechanics.md). Platform engineering reports do not justify universal reach penalties or optimal posting schedules.

The delivered post is clean paste-ready text, with factual gaps and material omissions in notes when needed. Preparing copy does not publish, schedule, comment, or contact anyone. See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
