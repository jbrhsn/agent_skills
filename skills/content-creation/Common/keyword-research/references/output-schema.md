# Report content

Use [the template](../assets/kresearch_template.md) as a starting point, omitting irrelevant sections rather than filling empty quotas.

Include input/source identity, research date, reader and intent, actual seeds/entities, locale/site settings, and sources queried. Topic-only research should say recommendations are provisional.

For recommended phrases, record intent, source/provenance, optional heuristic score, relevance to the article, and a natural placement. Link observed evidence or identify the retained raw observation; invented terms are editorial inference. Explain the primary choice without promising a ranking outcome.

Report Wikipedia pageviews only for the resolved article and actual measured window/access filters. The bundled `all` and `entity` modes emit a mean, not a time series; they cannot support “rising/falling” claims without additional dated observations. Missing data is unavailable, not measured zero.

If real community questions are used, retain source attribution and avoid copying unnecessary text. Source votes describe that question, not search volume.

Tags and hashtags are suggestions unless a named source measures their behavior. No fixed count or broad/narrow ratio is required beyond actual platform limits checked when needed.

The source log distinguishes success with observations, empty, blocked/failed, partial, and not queried as evidence allows. The script may conflate empty and unavailable in diagnostics; record that uncertainty rather than diagnosing a cause it did not expose.

End with material gaps, manual ranking changes, and useful next actions. Do not claim paid tools are the only possible evidence source; user analytics may answer a different, narrower question.
