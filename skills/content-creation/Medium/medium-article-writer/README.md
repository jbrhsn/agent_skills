# Medium Article Writer

Develop or revise long-form articles from notes, drafts, supplied text, or research briefs. Supports tutorials, explanations, arguments, case studies, and experience reports without forcing a technical persona or personal-story format.

Full folder workflows produce exactly two files: `medium_article.md` containing the article and `medium_publish.md` containing publishing guidelines. The latter records the confirmed direction and research rationale, subtitle and tags, image recommendations and placement, publishing time/timezone, sources, and publication checks. Complex briefs stay in the conversation and useful decisions go into the publishing notes; a separate brief is created only if requested. Explicit output preferences take precedence, and narrow edits need no extra artifact set.

For a new article or substantial rewrite, the skill generates five title, opening-hook, and structure proposals, then researches relevant Medium performance evidence. It compares all five and recommends the closest supported fit without changing the author's message, ideas, voice, or style. It presents source-linked reasoning and limitations, then waits for author confirmation and suggestions before writing the article or output files. Narrow edits and already confirmed directions do not restart the review.

Research distinguishes measured performance from advice and anecdotes; unavailable web access or weak evidence is disclosed, and any fallback recommendation is labeled editorial judgment. External evidence remains distinct from lived experience, and facts are never fabricated. Length, titles, summaries, and closing questions follow the article's purpose.

See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for reference routing. [Medium mechanics](references/medium-mechanics.md) covers packaging and links current disclosure, distribution, and paywall guidance. A polished draft is not a guarantee of policy eligibility; generated writing should not be disguised as mere spelling assistance.

The final artifact identifies material unresolved claims and publication checks. Drafting does not authorize submission or publishing.
