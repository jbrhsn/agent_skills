# Platform planning

Official-source research checked 2026-09-19. These sources establish capabilities and constraints, not guaranteed performance. Recheck changing requirements when they affect a concrete plan; account type, client, location, and access may matter. Load only the platform sections relevant to the request.

## LinkedIn

Plan around the professional reader's problem and the author's credible contribution. Text posts, documents, articles, newsletters, and media can serve different purposes; the presence of a writer skill for text posts does not mean all formats are covered by it. Clarify member profile versus organization Page when capability or analytics differences matter.

[Creator analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a597877) includes content and audience information. [Post analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1466099) describes available categories and differing retention windows. Use supplied account evidence to refine topics and formats; unknown private metrics stay unknown. Old Page marketing guidance is not a universal cadence rule for individuals.

[Member post scheduling](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1347212) and [Page scheduling](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548192/scheduled-posts-for-linkedin-pages) have different constraints. A calendar's target date does not establish that the chosen format can be scheduled using the user's tool. Check when scheduling feasibility matters; preparation of the plan itself does not schedule posts.

## Medium

Choose article depth from the reader promise and available material. Plan research, original examples, revision, and any visuals into capacity. [Distribution guidance](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473-Medium-s-Distribution-Guidelines-How-curators-review-stories-for-Boost-General-and-Network-Distribution) distinguishes Network, General, and Boost distribution; eligibility and curation are not promised outcomes. It also identifies AI-generated content as ineligible for General Distribution. Do not assume an AI-assisted workflow's final article qualifies for distribution; check applicable policies when that outcome matters, and do not suggest concealing AI involvement.

[Publishing guidance](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/225168768-Writing-and-publishing-your-first-story) covers topics, preview packaging, and scheduling. [Publication submissions](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213904978-How-to-submit-a-story-to-a-publication) depend on each publication's requirements. Treat acceptance and editorial timing as external dependencies, not dates the author controls. Search-oriented evergreen writing, existing followers, and publication audiences are possible paths, not reach guarantees.

## X

Plan concise observations, developed single posts, or threads according to the material. Short, medium, and long describe editorial depth, not guaranteed account-compatible lengths. [Post types](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/types-of-posts) describes Premium longer posts up to 25,000 characters and limitations on web scheduling. [Posting instructions](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post) also mention 4,000 characters in some client flows. Record capability uncertainty and verify the target client when a longer post is required; standard posts or [threads](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/create-a-thread) are alternatives when appropriate.

[Articles](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/articles) are a separate format with eligibility requirements; they are not synonymous with longer single posts and are outside the existing `x-post-writer` skill's stated formats. Flag a separate drafting assignment when planning one. Exact character checks belong with final copy, including every published thread component.

[View counts](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/view-counts) can include repeat and author views. Do not label public views as unique people or deduplicated cross-platform reach. Use available account data with its actual definitions and state access gaps.

## Other platforms and shared decisions

Apply the same audience, evidence, workload, and measurement method to other platforms. Research unfamiliar formats or constraints before promising feasibility, and identify gaps in available writing support. Cross-platform reuse should change the treatment while preserving meaning and attribution. There is no universal best time, posting quota, hashtag count, or content ratio in this skill; proposed tests should be tied to the user's audience and capacity.
