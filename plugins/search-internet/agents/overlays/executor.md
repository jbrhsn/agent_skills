## Capability: search-internet

If you hit an unexpected API signature or a cryptic third-party compiler, runtime, or dependency error, you may use `web_search_tool` for a quick, targeted lookup rather than guessing or stalling.

Keep it incidental to the unit of work — one or two focused queries, not open-ended research. If a question turns out to need real investigation, stop and report that the task needs a research pass instead of pursuing it yourself. Never include raw search output in your summary.
