# Review prompts

Use the checks relevant to the deliverable. Scale review to the size and stakes of the change; this is not a mandatory sequence or a formatting scorecard.

## Coverage and continuity

- Does the material deliver the requested outcome at the learner's level?
- Are requested topics and depths covered, with prerequisites taught or referenced?
- Are relevant brief, tier, navigation, and metadata relationships consistent?
- Are unfinished instructional placeholders removed while learner response fields remain usable?
- Are existing notes and progress preserved, and any completion claims accurate?

## Teaching quality

- Can the reader follow the reasoning and identify the important ideas?
- Are unfamiliar terms introduced with enough context for this audience?
- Do examples expose the important steps rather than merely restate an abstraction?
- Are simplifications bounded where a literal reading would mislead?
- Do exercises assess what was taught, with suitable support and answer placement?
- Are sections, headings, and visuals useful at this document's length?

A takeaway, analogy, misconception, trade-off, glossary, or recall question is useful when it improves understanding. Its absence is not itself a defect.

## Accuracy and evidence

- Are changing, uncertain, attributed, or consequential claims verified as required by the task and environment?
- Are sources actually consulted, and do they support the associated claims?
- Are hypothetical examples, expected outputs, and illustrative numbers distinguished from observations?
- Are verification limits disclosed where they affect the reader's conclusions?

## Domain and file checks

- Technical: are snippets identified as runnable code, excerpts, or pseudocode, with meaningful setup and results where needed?
- Craft: does analysis connect choices to effects, without presenting taste as a universal rule?
- Practice: are examples realistic about constraints and research limits, without invented learner experience or guaranteed outcomes?
- Exam: are official format and syllabus claims supported, and original questions labelled as practice?
- Assessment files: are model solutions separated from learner answers and logs?
- Writing angles: is required evidence distinguished from evidence already available?

Repair material defects within scope. Report what remains uncertain instead of inventing evidence or claiming that every check passed. If the opening overpromises, improve the content or correct the promise to match the requested scope.
