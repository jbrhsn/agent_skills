# Content Strategy

Research ideas and turn them into feasible weekly or monthly plans for LinkedIn, Medium, X, or multiple platforms. The skill includes discovery, evidence evaluation, prioritization, editorial pillars, calendar planning, repurposing, and a measurement plan. Other platforms can use the same method with researched constraints.

## Example requests

- “Plan next week's LinkedIn content for junior data engineers. I have three hours for preparation.”
- “Build an October Medium plan from these notes, with one article each week.”
- “Research a month of content across LinkedIn, Medium, and X using eight hours per week. My X account has no Premium.”
- “Find useful evergreen ideas for this audience; I don't need dates yet.”
- “Revise the last two weeks of this plan using these results and preserve published items.”

## Outputs and independence

Saved plans use `content_strategy.md`, with optional `content_research.md` for substantial evidence. Inline responses and custom filenames are supported. The calendar links to self-contained briefs with source status, production effort, dependencies, and author input needed. Monthly plans cover the full requested month; times and workload estimates are labeled when provisional.

Idea research is integrated directly. The separate `idea-research` skill remains available for focused discovery and its optional fetchers; it is not a dependency. Neither account access nor analytics is required. Existing research, analytics, drafts, and strategy documents can improve the plan without becoming prerequisites.

The [workflow](SKILL.md) uses [platform guidance](references/platform-planning.md), [calendar conventions](references/calendar-schema.md), and optional [handoffs](references/handoffs.md) to writer skills. Research and scores do not predict reach. The workflow prepares plans and briefs; publication and scheduling require a separate requested action.

## Validation

A second exercise produced a two-post LinkedIn week within 90 minutes using supplied notes only. It declined to label an unsupported angle as trending, kept examples illustrative, checked dates, and distinguished unequal observation windows.

Exercised in an independent planning session on 2026-09-19 for October content across Medium, LinkedIn, and X with four preparation hours per week, no analytics, and no X Premium. The result covered five publishing weeks, counted adaptation effort, identified a 20-hour total with no contingency, preserved source uncertainty, and provided independent briefs and publication dependencies. Skill validation, local-link and whitespace checks, and the five-platform sync dry-run passed. This exercise validates observed planning behavior, not publishing performance or account-specific scheduling.
