# Posting Notes

Fill every section. Delete a section only when it genuinely does not apply, and say why in one line rather than leaving it blank.

---

## Template

```markdown
# Posting Notes — [short post title, for the author's reference only]

## Topic tag
**Primary:** [the one niche this post belongs to, e.g. "LLM evaluation"]
**Secondary:** [related topic, or "none"]

Track this across posts. The retrieval layer builds topical authority from consistency, so two or three recurring tags outperform a scattered spread.

## At a glance
- **Characters:** [count] (target band 1,200–1,600)
- **Hook type:** [type used, from hook-frameworks.md]
- **Body pattern:** [pattern used, from structure-and-format.md]
- **Format:** Text post
- **Emojis:** [count] · **Bold phrases:** [count] · **Hashtags:** [count or none]

## First comment
[If source.md contained URLs, paste the ready-to-post first comment here — a short line of context plus the link. Links in the post body cut reach 50–70%, so they belong here.]

[If there were no links: "No links in source — no first comment needed."]

## Timing
- **Suggested day:** [Tue–Thu default]
- **Suggested window:** [time range] in [timezone, and how it was inferred — audience geography from source.md, or author's local time]
- **Why:** the first 60–90 minutes determine whether distribution expands. Post when you can reply for the next 90 minutes; author availability beats an optimal slot.

## First 90 minutes
- Reply to every comment, ideally within the first two hours — author replies lift lifetime engagement substantially.
- Reply with substance. Multi-reply threads carry more weight than single comments, so answer in a way that invites a follow-up.
- Do not solicit reciprocal engagement. Coordinated engagement patterns are detected and penalized.

## Gaps and flags
[Anything the post needed but source.md didn't have — a missing number, an unverified claim, a stat that would strengthen the argument. Nothing here is fabricated in the post; list it so the author can supply it before publishing. If a claim is strong and unsourced, say so.]

[If nothing: "No gaps — every claim traces to source.md."]

## Tradeoff made
[One line naming the main judgment call in the draft: which angle was chosen and what was cut, so the author can push back.]

## Repurposing
[Optional, one line. Whether this material would work better or additionally as a carousel/document post — those hold the highest engagement rates because each swipe adds dwell time. Only suggest it when the content is genuinely visual, enumerable, or step-based.]
```

---

## Notes on filling this

- **Be specific in "Gaps and flags."** This is the section that protects the author from publishing something they can't defend. Vague entries make it useless.
- **The tradeoff line is not optional.** Every draft discards a viable angle; naming it makes revision cheap.
- **Never recommend engagement pods, comment-for-comment exchanges, or bait tactics** anywhere in this file.
- Keep the whole file scannable. It's a pre-flight check, not an essay.
